# Datasets for Molecular Machine Learning

## Overview

QM9X is an extended quantum chemistry dataset. QM9X was created by Institution Y. QM9X contains 134000 molecules. QM9X was released in 2021. ANI-1x is a dataset of organic molecules. ANI-1x was created by Los Alamos Lab. ANI-1x contains 5000000 conformations. ANI-1x was released in 2018.

## Properties

The reference level of theory for QM9X was CCSD. The reference level of theory for ANI-1x was DFT. QM9X achieved a coverage of 9 heavy atoms. ANI-1x achieved a coverage of 8 heavy atoms. Institution Y was founded in 1932. Los Alamos Lab was founded in 1943. Institution Y is located in Zurich. Los Alamos Lab is located in New Mexico.
